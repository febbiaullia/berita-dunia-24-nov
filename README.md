# The World Today — 24 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, for the
make.fun bounty **news-image-20261124** ("Sum up 24 November's world news in
one image").

## The stories in the picture

- **Huang is back** — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as president and CEO,
  11 days after he resigned; the board voted unanimously after input from
  senior levels of the US government, and the shares jumped about 31%.
  Source: Nvidia / WSJ / CNN / TechCrunch, 24 Nov (Asia-Pacific morning).
- **Drone over Poland** — Polish armed forces say a Russian drone briefly
  violated Polish airspace during the overnight attack on western Ukraine;
  the shoot-down was authorised but held off, and NATO will send an
  AWACS early-warning aircraft to Poland. Source: Reuters, 24 Nov.
- **World Cup hosts named** — FIFA appoints the USA, Mexico, Costa Rica
  and Jamaica as hosts of the 2031 Women's World Cup (the first with 48
  teams) and England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland for 2035.
  Source: FIFA, 24 Nov (Asia-Pacific morning).
- **Chess crown on the line** — Defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju meets
  challenger Javokhir Sindarov in Game 1 of the World Championship in
  Geneva, the youngest title match in history. Source: Chess.com / FIDE,
  24 Nov.
- **Also today:** Indonesia confirms 24 dead in the Cikande candy-factory
  fire, with about 30 people still missing (Reuters); a new Oxfam/CEOBS
  report finds the war in Gaza has left toxic contamination across the
  Strip; PM Modi opens an AI centre at IIT Patna.

## How it runs

The poster is a single 1024x1024 PNG held in this repository (275,120 bytes,
sha256 `2c17d88396c93c392c965966005899a6d1d7ca3cd126156b11f88d8ae7606974`).
The program copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
